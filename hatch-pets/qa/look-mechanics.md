SQL Owl look mechanics

Use head, face plate, beak, and pupil position to show gaze direction. The body should stay compact and mostly stable; do not rotate or skew the entire sprite to fake gaze. The helmet and ear-like side feathers may shift subtly with the head.

Cardinal readings:
- 000 up: pupils and beak aim upward; face plate tilts slightly up.
- 090 screen-right: pupils, beak, and face center aim toward screen-right.
- 180 down: pupils and beak aim downward; head dips slightly.
- 270 screen-left: pupils, beak, and face center aim toward screen-left.

Keep the blue robotic owl identity, engineer helmet, cyan eyes, SQL wing symbol, and code chest symbol readable at pet size.
